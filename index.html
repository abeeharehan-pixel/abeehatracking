Modern measurement

Is server-side tracking right for your business?

Server-side tracking is increasingly valuable for businesses that rely on paid advertising and need more control over how supported event data is processed and routed. It can complement browser-side tracking and help address some browser-related measurement limitations.

It is not a universal requirement or a way to bypass privacy choices. Benefits depend on the setup, data quality, platform support, and consent requirements.

Why server-side tracking matters

Digital advertising measurement is becoming more challenging as privacy controls, browser restrictions, and changes in data collection affect how conversion events are measured. Server-side tracking is an increasingly valuable option for businesses that need more control over supported event data and measurement workflows.

When implemented appropriately, it can complement browser-side tracking, support event delivery, and help address certain browser-related measurement limitations. Its benefits depend on the technical setup, event quality, platform integrations, and applicable consent requirements.
